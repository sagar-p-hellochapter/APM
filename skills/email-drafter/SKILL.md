# email-drafter

Seventh skill. Renders **on-demand** email drafts in the portal — you tap **Generate**, review,
and Copy into Outlook. Nothing sends automatically (external email is draft-only,
ARCHITECTURE.md §2); the only address the system is ever allowed to auto-send to is yours.

Implemented in the portal as `buildDraft(type, ctx)` + a draft preview modal (Copy / Close).
No data file of its own — it composes from the item you tapped plus the client on the project.

## Generate buttons (where they appear)
| Button | Where | Type | Recipient |
|--------|-------|------|-----------|
| ✉ Reminder to me | Today task (overdue/today/2-day) | `task-reminder` | you |
| ✉ Bill client | Today milestone · Payments milestone | `milestone-bill` | client |
| ✉ Reminder to me | Payments sub (overdue/due-today/due-soon) | `sub-reminder` | you |
| ✉ CO notice to client | Changes → CO detail | `co-notice` | client |

(The weekly client update is generated by `weekly-update-drafter` with its own Copy button.)

## Behavior
- **Recipient**: self drafts → `sagar.p@hellochapter.com`; client drafts → the backfilled
  client email, or "— (confirm recipient)" when unknown (333 E 43rd, 330 E 79th).
- **Signature**: `SIGNATURE` constant (defaults to "Sagar Patel / Project Manager, Chapter");
  swap for your real block, matching `config/settings.json`.
- **CO notice** follows SOP A9 — states cost, and prompts you to confirm schedule + lead-time
  impact, and that no work proceeds until signed + paid.
- Every draft ends up on the clipboard via **Copy email** for pasting into Outlook.
